X Group Tab — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

X Group Tab is a browser extension that helps you count, group, merge and close your open tabs. This policy explains what data the extension touches.

Data we collect

None. X Group Tab does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or browsing data. It has no servers, no analytics and no third-party code, and it makes no network requests.

Data processed locally

  • Tabs and Tab Groups (tabs, tabGroups permissions): the extension reads the URL, title and favicon of your open tabs and your Tab Groups only while the popup is open, to show counts per site and to group, move or close tabs when you click a button. This information never leaves your browser and is not stored.
  • Settings (storage permission): your chosen interface language and view mode (“By site” / “By group”) are saved with chrome.storage.sync, which your browser may sync to your own Google account if Chrome sync is enabled.
